# Kitchen decisions — proposed

Date: 2026-10-22. Owner: project owner for business decisions; primary agent for planning. [Scope](spec.md); [authorization and handoff](phase-tracker.md).

All recommendations below are unapproved. Accepted OR-01 capture is not approval to prepare food, expose customer details, change an order or claim payment. Answers must be recorded here before dependent implementation; specification approval and source authorization are separate.

| ID | Options and consequence | Recommendation / decision needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| KT-D01 — Dispatch timing | Automatic on capture is one less action but sends every saved order straight to preparation and changes OR-01 meaning. Explicit dispatch separates storage from the decision to prepare but requires an operator action. Payment-gated dispatch avoids unpaid preparation but depends on unplanned Billing/payment evidence. | Explicit dispatch of a captured order by current authorized staff/admin. Separately approve whether preparation before payment is allowed. If payment is mandatory, hold dispatch implementation until Billing provides an approved trusted eligibility boundary; do not accept a client `paid` flag or build temporary payment fields. No default inferred. |
| KT-D02 — Kitchen-visible data | Full Orders readback is easy to reuse but exposes customer name/phone, money and policy metadata. A preparation projection minimizes exposure but must retain all composition and instructions. Customer name may aid collection but collection is outside this increment. | Order ID/reference, capture/dispatch times, takeaway type, ordered lines/quantities, captured item/size/extra/combo names and component quantities, order/line preparation instructions. Exclude customer name/phone, prices/totals/currency, capture policy and retry request. Free text may itself contain personal data: confirm kitchen staff may see the captured instructions and operational instructions must discourage personal data. This is no privacy/retention compliance claim. |
| KT-D03 — Status and permission ownership | Reusing Orders `captured` for preparation would mix distinct lifecycles. Kitchen-specific roles could restrict access but require a new approved permission model. Existing staff/admin is the smallest existing policy, granting all such staff preparation access. | Kitchen owns whole-ticket `queued → preparing → ready`; Orders remains immutable `captured`. Current branch-authorized staff/admin may dispatch, read and advance. No backward transitions, line statuses, served/collected/completed or payment effects. Approve both transitions and broad existing-role access; narrower access changes scope before source work. |
| KT-D04 — Amendments and cancellation | Immutable tickets are the smallest bounded development slice. Pre-start withdrawal adds a race with preparing; post-start cancellation requires acknowledgement and policy for work already done. Editing an order silently would erase what the kitchen saw. | Defer all amend/cancel APIs in the first development slice, including before dispatch, preserving accepted OR-01 immutability. Do not simulate cancellation by deleting tickets, marking ready, or creating an undocumented replacement order. Approve this development-only boundary; if cancellation is needed now, rescope before implementation with actor, allowed states, race outcome and kitchen acknowledgement. A separately approved correction/cancellation workflow is required before restaurant operation. |
