# Billing/Payments — decisions awaiting approval

Date: 2026-10-25. All recommendations are **proposed**. Owner: project owner; accountant/business and payment-provider input where applicable. No legal applicability/rate/mandate is determined by this plan. Existing [compliance guidance](../../compliance/README.md) owns source/applicability policy; its historical links are not newly verified authorities.

| ID / question | Recommended smallest sound option | Alternatives / consequence | Blocking gate and required decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BP-D01 — methods, full payment and cash handling | One full positive cash payment; operator attests receipt of exact payable. No tendered/change fields, partial payments, credit, tips or external terminals | Recording tender/change better supports counter operation but adds invariants. External-terminal or gateway acceptance needs outcome verification, uncertain-result recovery and reconciliation; no card data collection | BP-01: owner must approve cash-only development slice, exact-received semantics and exclusion of zero-payable sales, or request a revised scope. No PAN/CVV/PIN/track storage |
| BP-D02 — payable and fiscal policy | Block settlement without explicit validated policy; calculate at settlement from captured merchandise and approved versioned fiscal configuration, then freeze all inputs/results | Treating missing tax as zero or merchandise as payable invents policy. A generic tax engine is unjustified. Snapshot at capture would change accepted Orders and needs a migration/scope decision | BP-00/BP-01: owner/accountant must resolve applicability, inclusive/exclusive captured prices, rate/category/effective timing, rounding/allocation and any method-dependent treatment. Confirm business/branch ownership, identifiers and configuration provisioning. If pending, only approve synthetic development fixtures, explicitly disabling operational settlement; that is a limited development scope, not approval of tax policy |
| BP-D03 — Kitchen/payment sequencing | Accept settlement at any preparation state or before dispatch; retain explicit independent dispatch and no automatic paid/collected state | Prepayment enforcement would change accepted Kitchen admission; pay-on-ready would introduce a new dependency. Neither is required to prove one cash record | BP-01: owner approves independent states for development. Restaurant workflow, handover and collection rules remain a release decision |
| BP-D04 — receipts, identity and numbering | BP-01 provides an internal bill/payment record with opaque stable identity only, no fiscal number allocation, print status or legal-receipt claim | Human receipt numbering/layout and fiscal integration may be required for applicable operation, but cannot be guessed. Printing is not proven by saved data | BP-01: owner explicitly accepts internal-record-only development scope. Before affected release, owner/accountant supplies applicable receipt/invoice fields, numbering, registration, issue timing, retention and any online authority requirements with current authoritative evidence; hardware output separately scoped |
| BP-D05 — mistakes, voids and refunds | Immutable original settlement; no edit/delete/refund API in BP-01. Before restaurant operation, scope explicit linked reversal/refund records with reason, verified actor, authorization and duplicate/over-refund protection | Full refund only is the simplest later correction candidate; partial refunds/returns and tax correction need further decisions. Never overwrite/delete history or compensate through Orders/Kitchen state | BP-01: owner accepts technical slice without correction operations. Before affected release: distinguish pre-settlement cancellation from post-payment refund, approve who may act, cash repayment proof, limits and applicable fiscal corrections; do not label this product P1 |
| BP-D06 — roles, retained data and recovery | Reuse current staff/admin policy for settlement/read, retain minimal bill data and internal actor attribution; no customer copying or new RBAC | Admin-only settlement is simpler to restrict but may block cashier workflow. Customer fields may be needed by confirmed fiscal rules; no assumed retention period | BP-01: owner approves staff/admin access and proposed allowlist, or specifies narrower access. Operational retention/privacy/backup/loss-recovery policy must be resolved before affected release |

BP-00 also finishes concrete application/HTTP/schema contracts after these choices: response capacity, exact retry comparison, error mappings, supported synthetic policy examples, calculation bounds and minimal configuration interface. Routine technical detail is not a second business approval ritual, but cannot silently fill an unresolved material policy choice.
