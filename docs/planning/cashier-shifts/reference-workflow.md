# Cashier Shifts — reference workflow and integration options

Status: reference for CS-00, 2026-10-28. User-confirmed decisions are explicitly marked below; detailed behavior, API migration and architecture direction remain proposed. [Discovery and phase tracker](discovery.md); [global tracker](../TRACKER.md).

## Reference day for the first release

Confirmed product requirements, 2026-10-28: each cashier has a separate drawer; multiple cashiers can operate concurrently; drawer handover and cash movements are supported; new cash payments require an open drawer session; restaurant settings support cashier self-close and administrator/designated-replacement close. Proposed model: identify each drawer/session and bind each payment and cash movement to the intended open drawer. One open session per drawer is a candidate safeguard, not yet an approved rule. Whether drawers are assigned to a person, a workstation or a physical device, and detailed handover/replacement rules remain open; do not infer a permanent cashier–drawer assignment.

1. **Open.** An authorized staff member counts and declares the cash already in the drawer. The system records the amount, currency and opener; it does not assume yesterday's closing balance is today's float. Proposed access: existing staff; confirm who actually opens and supplies float.
2. **Collect.** Each cashier records full cash payments against the intended open drawer session, with the verified payment actor retained. Concurrent drawers do not share a balance. One committed payment has one matching attribution in the same transaction. A lost response is recovered with the original request identity. The current Billing slice records exact-received full cash, not tendered amount or change. Exact assignment and authorization remain to be decided.
3. **Move cash.** Record positive cash-in or cash-out with amount, reason, verified actor and time, such as float top-ups, supplier expenses and safe drops. Proposed first-release scope: a simple signed movement record with mandatory reason, no generic accounting engine. Operators must validate which movements occur, who can make them and whether approval or supporting references are needed. Cash-out should not be silently classified as a sale or refund. Correct an erroneous movement with a linked reversal/new entry, retaining the original; no deletion. Prevent duplicate recording on retry and define the treatment of withdrawals exceeding the calculated balance before contracts are approved.
4. **Close.** An authorized closer counts cash and records the count. Expected = opening float + cash sales + cash in − cash out; variance = counted − expected. Record a nonzero variance and explanation, not a fabricated zero. Sessions may cross midnight; closing is explicit. Example in consistent currency units: 1,000 opening + 5,000 sales + 200 top-up − 500 safe drop = 5,700 expected; 5,600 counted yields −100 variance.
5. **Handover.** Proposed simple flow: the current custodian initiates transfer to an identified eligible cashier; the incoming cashier counts and explicitly accepts. Record both actors, time, counted amount and any difference; keep the same open drawer session and its financial history, with a custody boundary for attribution. During a pending transfer, prevent new cash activity until acceptance or cancellation; atomic completion changes custodian once. Do not imply both cashiers may simultaneously use the same drawer. Payment or movement racing handover must commit under the old custodian before transfer or fail and require refresh; identical retries recover the original result. This is a candidate protocol, not a user-approved requirement. A missing outgoing cashier needs a separately defined authorized recovery path; do not forge their acknowledgment or silently change custody.
6. **Closing policy and unavailable closer.** Restaurant settings must support cashier self-close and administrator/designated-replacement close, as the user directed. Proposed simple administration: an administrator changes a branch-applicable business policy, with current authorization, recorded actor and revision; apply the current policy to a close in the same transaction. Exact allowed modes, combination/default, and designated replacement management are not yet decided. Current Access has staff and administrator only: giving a replacement administrator privileges grants broader access, so a narrow explicit capability or other reviewed mechanism needs design. Record the actual verified closer. If nobody authorized is available, keep the session open and use an approved recovery procedure; do not auto-close, share credentials or manufacture consent.
7. **Correct mistakes.** An authorized reviewer records a linked, attributed correction with reason; original close, count, payment and movement history remain visible. Correcting a count and correcting a payment assigned to the wrong session have different effects: the latter may affect both sessions and must never duplicate or alter the bill. Corrections after close need explicit adjustment/reconciliation semantics, authorization and evidence before the affected workflow launches. This is a product/operational launch blocker, not a claim that a first isolated backend increment must implement every correction.

Remaining product decisions: how drawers are assigned/selected, exact handover and unavailable-cashier recovery, closing settings and default, replacement designation and movement authorization. Operator validation is still needed for actual setup, movement categories, custody and correction practices. Revisit unconfirmed assumptions as evidence arrives; no need to delay all CS-00 design while arranging validation.

## Integration evidence and candidate approaches

Source facts, inspected 2026-10-28: [AccessService](../../../apps/api/src/modules/authentication/application/access.service.ts) runs authorization and awaited callback in one transaction; [PostgresAccessStore](../../../apps/api/src/modules/authentication/infrastructure/access-store.ts) acquires a fixed installation-wide PostgreSQL transaction advisory lock. [TransactionContext](../../../apps/api/src/infrastructure/database/transaction.ts) is opaque and valid only during that transaction. [BillingService](../../../apps/api/src/modules/billing/application/billing.service.ts) normalizes input before `runAuthorized`, checks successful retry before current policy and insert, and writes its own aggregate. [Billing store](../../../apps/api/src/modules/billing/application/billing-store.ts) has no public cross-module settlement operation; [BillingModule](../../../apps/api/src/modules/billing/billing.module.ts) exports no providers and is absent from [AppModule](../../../apps/api/src/app.module.ts). The [controller](../../../apps/api/src/modules/billing/http/billing.controller.ts) calls BillingService directly; existing test fixtures also instantiate it directly.

| Candidate | Transaction and module consequences | Strengths and open evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A. Billing calls a Shifts public operation | Billing remains transaction owner via `runAuthorized`; the new Shifts operation receives its authorized scope and records attribution in that transaction. Close must use the same admission/locking rule. Billing imports Shifts, which must not import Billing. | Smallest apparent change to the current settlement entry point; Shifts needs a minimal owned cash entry. Requires Nest/provider export design, safe rollback/error mapping, explicit handling of replay before new-session admission and proof of close/settle ordering. |
| B. Composition service coordinates Billing and Shifts | A higher-level service owns one `runAuthorized` transaction and calls transaction-scoped public operations of both modules. Refactor Billing's existing service to expose a bounded operation without a nested authorization call; route composition must invoke the coordinator. | Explicit orchestration without Billing→Shifts dependency; more refactoring and risk of accidentally leaving a settlement path that bypasses attribution. Existing direct tests and isolated composition must be adapted. Coordinator must preserve replay precedence and 201/Location behavior. |
| C. Billing owns association; Shifts reads a Billing projection | Billing writes the shift association as part of its aggregate; Shifts closes using a new narrow Billing public aggregate/summary operation in the same authorized transaction. Shifts imports Billing; Billing needs a safe session admission mechanism without creating the reverse dependency. | One authoritative payment/association record, but session admission and close query are harder to compose without a cycle or coordinator; define ownership and bounded aggregate query without exposing Billing rows. Source currently provides neither interface. |

All three require one transaction for payment and attribution, server-trusted scope, exactly-once retry handling and a shared close/settlement ordering protocol. No option is selected. A best-effort asynchronous event or after-commit update cannot establish the required invariant and is excluded. The fixed advisory lock can serialize participants **if every relevant mutation acquires it through the same Access path**, but source inspection alone does not prove the new path does so; it also serializes unrelated Access work across the installation. Assess lock duration and ordering, unique constraints, rollback and real contention in later authorized verification. No nested `runAuthorized`, second connection or external I/O inside the transaction. Evaluate any finer-grained locking separately; do not assume it is necessary or safe.

## Proposed Billing compatibility matrix

These are targets for contract review, not available behavior or an approved migration. The accepted [Billing contract](../../../apps/api/src/modules/billing/docs/features/settlement/spec.md#financial-and-persistence-invariants) structurally normalizes the existing eight-field request before lookup, then compares its canonical stored request; its DTO currently has no shift field. A required new field would reject old retries before lookup unless validation and version-aware matching are deliberately designed.

| Situation | Proposed outcome | Compatibility/design work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Historical successful request with original body/key, including after a shift closes | Under current authorization, replay original bill; no new attribution or invented old session | Accept and normalize the legacy shape through replay lookup; preserve its exact historical comparison and 201/Location. Test old stored fixtures and changed-content conflicts. |
| New settlement without shift identity | Reject with a clear actionable response and no writes, if D05 is approved | This changes accepted admission and breaks old new-payment clients; choose versioned route/contract or a reviewed compatible transition. Historical replay must remain distinguishable from new work. |
| Uncommitted request naming closed/replaced session | Conflict without bill or attribution; refresh and explicitly reconfirm the intended session | Define session identity, response and recovery; never silently redirect money to a new session. |
| Successful new request retried after close/new opening | Replay original bill after current authorization, with no second attribution | Retry lookup and comparison must precede current open-session admission; changed content conflicts. |
| Billing isolated composition | Contract tests compose the candidate integration explicitly; AppModule remains unchanged | Current Billing source already has isolated HTTP and direct-service test compositions. Update both to cover the chosen composition without introducing a bypass; no live route activation follows. |
| Existing bill readback | Return saved BillView, including legacy records; do not reprice | Preserve current known-order GET and response shape unless a separately approved migration is justified. |

No external client inventory was established by source search; internal test/service callers are known. Before approval identify actual consumers and select migration/versioning and safe error/status details with the [REST skill](../../../.agents/skills/rest-api-contracts/SKILL.md), canonical convention and shared HTTP wiring. Prior Billing evidence proves its accepted slice, not the new composition or race.

## Scope choices and next evidence

Recommend owner review of the candidate handover, closing-policy modes and designated replacement mechanism, and correction scope for first backend increment versus operational release. Define acceptance for concurrent separate drawers, wrong-drawer rejection, handover/payment races, retry, policy changes, revoked replacement access, movement/close races, insufficient calculated balance and count corrections before source approval. Cash-count/attribution correction must be resolved before restaurant launch even if delivered later.

CS-00 can proceed with proposed contracts and compare these candidates before operator validation is complete; record assumptions and revisit them when evidence arrives. Source selection and implementation remain gated on agreed scope, migration contract and a defensible concurrency design.
